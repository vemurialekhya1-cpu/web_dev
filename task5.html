KOREAN KANAKA RAJU

ACTION,DRAMA AND REVANGE

LANGUAGE

Director:MERALAPAKALA GANDHI

RELEASE date:8 August 2026

⭐⭐⭐⭐3.5/5

The story follows Kanaka Raju, a young man from Rayalaseema whose life changes after he becomes connected to a mysterious Korean ghost. The story then takes him from his village to South Korea, mixing supernatural elements with comedy and action.

Our Review

Varun Tej gives an energetic performance, while Satya is one of the main highlights because of his comedy and timing. Ritika Nayak also performs well in her role. The Korean portions have stylish visuals and good cinematography.
