The first half is more lively, with comedy and entertainment, while the second half becomes slower in places. Reviews also pointed to an old-fashioned storyline and an ending that does not fully satisfy.Bhartha Mahasayulaku Wignyapthi is a family-oriented entertainer built around relationships, misunderstandings and comedy. Ravi Teja carries much of the film with his energetic performance, while the supporting cast adds several humorous moments. The music by Bheems Ceciroleo was also noted positively.

Our Review

A light family entertainer with good comedy moments and Ravi Teja's performance, but the predictable story and uneven second half reduce its impact.
