CHEENAI LOVE STORY

Action,love story and emotion

LANGUAGE

Director:Ravi Namburii

RELEASE date:24 july 2026

⭐⭐⭐⭐4/5

The story follows Steven Shankar and Niveditha, two young people who meet in Chennai. Their friendship gradually develops into love as they deal with personal struggles, emotions, and their past experiences. The performances are one of the film's strengths. Kiran Abbavaram and Sri Gouri Priya bring emotion and sincerity to their characters. The film also benefits from Mani Sharma's music and its Chennai setting.

Our Review

A youthful romantic drama with good performances, emotional moments, and pleasant music, though the long runtime and second-half writing may not appeal to everyone.
