⭐⭐⭐⭐3/5

Youth is a Tamil coming-of-age romantic comedy directed by Ken Karunaas, who also plays the lead role. The story follows Praveen, a teenager who experiences love, friendship, family expectations, and the ups and downs of growing up. The movie's biggest strengths are its comedy, youthful atmosphere, performances, and music. Ken Karunaas gives a natural performance, while the supporting actors add fun and emotion. GV Prakash Kumar's music also suits the energetic mood of the film.

Our Review

A light-hearted and youthful movie with comedy, friendship, romance, and family emotions. It is mainly aimed at young audiences.
