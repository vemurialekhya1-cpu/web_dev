GODAVARI GATTUPAINA

ACTION,DRAMA AND FAMILY

LANGUAGE

Director:Subash Chandra

RELEASE date:8 may 2026

⭐⭐⭐⭐3.5/5

The story follows Raju, an auto driver from a Godavari village, who falls in love with Maaya, a college student. Their relationship faces challenges because of family expectations, social differences, and religion.

Our Review

The story is simple and easy to follow, although some parts may feel predictable. Overall, it is a pleasant movie with good performances, beautiful visuals, and an emotional storyline.
