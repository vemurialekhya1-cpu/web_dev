LANGUAGE

Director:Nandini Reddy

RELEASE date:19 june 2026

⭐⭐⭐⭐4.5/5

The story follows Swarna, a woman who tries to build a peaceful family life while keeping her troubled past behind her. When dangers from her past return, she must protect her family and face her enemies. Samantha's performance is the main highlight of the movie. The action sequences are energetic, and the family emotions add another layer to the story. The supporting cast also contributes well, while the cinematography and background score support the action scenes..

Our Review

An entertaining action-drama with a strong lead performance, family emotions, and engaging action sequences.
